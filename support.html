Support

Plain PDF is free for everyone and stays free. It is made by one person in Serbia. If it helps you, three things help back:

  • Tell someone. A parent, a colleague, anyone tired of ads in PDF apps. That is how the app spreads.
  • Rate it on Google Play. A short review from a real person counts more than any advert.
  • Supporter purchase. In the Google Play version, Settings has an optional Supporter purchase that unlocks extra accent colours and icons. Every reading and editing feature is free regardless.

Need help?

Write to support@plainpdf.app and say which phone you have and what you were trying to do. If the app ever crashes, it offers a report on the next start; sharing it to that address helps a lot.
