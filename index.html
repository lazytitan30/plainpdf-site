Plain PDF

Free PDF reader and editor for Android. No ads, no account, no cloud.

Get it on Google Play

It cannot phone home. The app does not hold the internet permission at all, so your documents stay on your phone. The source code is public, so anyone can check.

What it does

  • Read, search, bookmark, fill in forms, draw and highlight
  • Sign documents with a drawn signature
  • Scan pages with the camera; the edges are found for you
  • Text recognition on the phone, offline, Latin and Cyrillic included, so scans can be searched
  • Merge, split, organise pages, compress, add page numbers, redact, write a note
  • Read aloud, print, share a single page
  • 17 languages

Made for people who want a PDF app that simply works: big buttons, no hidden gestures.
